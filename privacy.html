PixelPlay AI (“we”, “our”, or “us”), available as package com.pixelplayai.app, is an AI-powered video creation mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app and associated backend services.

1 Information We Collect

  1. Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, display name, and avatar image URL from Google to identify your account and maintain your credit balance.
  2. User Input & Generation Data: Text prompts, generation settings (duration, aspect ratio, model options), and any optional reference images you provide to generate videos.
  3. Generated Content: Video clips rendered through our service are stored securely in cloud storage so you can view, play, and download them within your gallery.
  4. Purchase & Transaction Data: Purchase tokens, order IDs, and credit pack transactions verified through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store credit card or payment credentials (processed directly by Google Play).
  5. Technical & Diagnostic Information: Basic device information (OS version, device model, app version code) and crash/error logs to diagnose technical issues and ensure stability.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide the Service: Authenticate you, track your credit balance, and execute video generation requests.
  • AI Processing: Transmit prompt inputs and reference media to specialized cloud AI infrastructure providers solely to compute and render the requested video clips.
  • Purchases & Verification: Verify legitimate Google Play in-app credit purchases and credit your account.
  • Support & Safety: Prevent abuse, detect unauthorized use or prohibited content, and debug service interruptions.

3 Third-Party Service Providers

We only share data with trusted third parties strictly necessary to operate our service:

  • Identity & Store Platforms: Google Identity Services and Google Play Billing for secure authentication and purchase verification.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Storage Providers: Secure enterprise cloud hosting and object storage providers for hosting APIs and storing your generated video clips safely.
  • Cloud AI Inference Providers: Specialized cloud compute partners used to run video generation models on prompt inputs. These providers process inputs strictly to fulfill generation requests and do not use your inputs or generated clips to train public foundation models without consent.

🛡️ We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or third-party advertisers under any circumstance.

4 Data Retention & Deletion

  • Retention: Account information and generation history are retained as long as your account remains active so you can access your videos and credits.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:
    • In-App: Via the “Delete Account” button directly inside the Account screen.
    • By Email: Send a deletion request to jithinag91reg@gmail.com.
    Upon receiving your request, your account record, email, and stored video files will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

5 Children's Privacy

PixelPlay AI is intended for users who are at least 13 years of age (or 16/18 where local legislation requires). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete that information.

6 Security

We implement industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and secure cloud storage access controls to protect your data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
