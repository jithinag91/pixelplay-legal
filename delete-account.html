Delete Your Account

Application: PixelPlay AI (com.pixelplayai.app) • Effective Date: November 8, 2026
Privacy Policy Terms & EULA Delete Account
This page explains how to delete your PixelPlay AI account (developer: PixelPlay AI, package com.pixelplayai.app) and all the data linked to it. You can do it inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

1 Delete in the app

  1. Open PixelPlay AI and sign in.
  2. Tap the Account tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Tap Confirm Permanent Deletion.

Your account and data are deleted right away. You are then signed out.

2 Delete by email

  1. Email jithinag91reg@gmail.com with the subject “PixelPlay AI account deletion”.
  2. Send it from the email address you sign in with, so we can find your account.
  3. We confirm by reply and delete your account within 30 days.

3 What we delete

  • Account details: name, email address and profile picture link.
  • Videos you made and their records.
  • Photos you uploaded, characters, character sheets and library assets.
  • Stories, scenes and series, including their text and images.
  • Your remaining credit balance. Unused credits are lost and are not refunded.

4 What we keep

We keep purchase records (Google Play order token, product and credits given), the credit history and AI usage cost logs. They contain no prompts, photos or videos. We keep them for accounting, tax and fraud prevention for up to 7 years, then delete them.

5 Delete only some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove data. In the app you can delete any video, character, asset, story or series on its own.
